# Rick&Max — QAI

Target duration: 60 seconds. 130 spoken words.

## Slide 1 · 0:00–0:16

A professor fills the board. Students copy. But who is lost, and why? The professor cannot read hundreds of answers. Students wait for feedback, and passive classes make attention difficult, especially for those of us with ADHD.

## Slide 2 · 0:16–0:30

QAI turns that class into active practice. Students scan a QR code, explain their reasoning or upload a photo, and get immediate, private feedback grounded in course material.

## Slide 3 · 0:30–0:47

Professors see what to teach next. In this synthetic example, two of six answers missed the same term. QAI surfaces the misconception and suggests an explanation and a fresh question, with the professor in control.

## Slide 4 · 0:47–1:00

Students practise instead of just watching. Professors teach to the gaps they can now see. We’re Rick and Max. QAI makes every answer a chance to improve the next lesson.
