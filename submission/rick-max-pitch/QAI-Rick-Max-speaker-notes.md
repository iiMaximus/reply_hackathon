# Rick&Max — QAI

Target duration: 60 seconds. 132 spoken words.

## Slide 1 · 0:00–0:14

Students watch someone fill a board. Professors cannot see where the class is stuck. Feedback arrives too late. For students like me with ADHD, passive classes are particularly hard to stay engaged in.

## Slide 2 · 0:14–0:34

QAI makes practice interactive. Professors upload worksheets, images or Markdown. Students join by QR, then type, upload a photo or draw on the whiteboard. AI gives private, course-grounded feedback. Professors get misconception reports and follow-up questions. Students keep a personal practice history.

## Slide 3 · 0:34–0:46

Here is the updated whiteboard: students sketch their working directly in the browser. They can undo, clear, add a note and submit their drawing.

## Slide 4 · 0:46–1:00

Here, two of six synthetic responses missed the same term. The professor sees what needs explaining, with a worked example and a fresh check. Rick and Max: feedback that changes the next lesson.
